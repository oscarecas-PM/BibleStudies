"Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path." Psalm 119:105

Why This Exists

I'm a Christian, a husband, and a father — and I'm still learning what all three of those mean. Scripture has been the foundation of that learning, but I've found that the best way to understand it isn't to read passively. It's to dig in, ask questions, sit with uncomfortable passages, and talk about them with the people I love.

These studies come from that process. Each one started as a conversation — a topic I was wrestling with, a passage I wanted to understand better, a question one of my kids asked that I didn't have a ready answer for. I built them to help me grow, and I share them because growth isn't meant to be solitary.

Built with AI, Shaped by People

Every study here was created using a Bible study skill I built for Claude Code. The AI surfaces passages, provides historical and linguistic context, and generates discussion questions — but it doesn't write the study. I do. The tool brings the raw material; I decide what resonates, what goes deeper, and what my audience needs to hear.

I believe technology should serve us — not distract us, not replace our thinking, but amplify what we're already trying to do. If you're trying to know God's word better, to lead a small group, to have deeper conversations with your family about faith — these tools can help you do more of that, not less. The goal isn't efficiency. It's depth.

My hope is that we learn to adopt technology in ways that make us more present, more peaceful, and more fulfilled — not more busy. That we use these tools to go deeper into what matters, not to skim the surface faster. Scripture deserves our full attention. Technology can help us give it.
